## When do you need a commercial license?

You can get a separate commercial license from the project owner if:

- You want to use the code or a derivative in a product or service **without releasing your source code**
- Your company cannot meet the terms of AGPL-3.0
- You need extra rights such as support, custom development or use of the brand

Personal projects, community sites and anyone keeping their source open under AGPL-3.0 **do not need** a commercial license.
